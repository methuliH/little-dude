Drop your little guy's art in this folder (PNG, GIF, WEBP or APNG — transparent background looks best).

Name files after moods and he'll switch between them:
  idle     - default, required if you only add one file
  happy, excited, curious, judging, sad, love, talk, sleepy

Example: idle.gif, sleepy.png, excited.gif
Any mood without a file falls back to idle. Restart the app after adding files.
